## Full description (4000 max)

Simple Body Log is a private tracker for your weight and body measurements.

- Log your weight in seconds
- Tap a part of the body map and slide the ruler to record a measurement
- Choose from 16 measurements, from neck, chest and waist to biceps, thighs and calves, and name up to 3 of your own
- Male or female body map
- See your progress on trend charts for your weight and every measurement
- Set a goal and see how far you've come since your start date
- A Health screen with BMI, waist-to-height and waist-to-hip ratios, with standard or Asian BMI ranges; pick which ones show
- Browse, edit and add past entries
- Kg or lb, cm or in
- Light and dark themes
- Import and export your data as CSV

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
