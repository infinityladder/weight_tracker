# Changelog

Release notes for Google Play. Paste the block for a version into the
"Release notes" box in Play Console. Each language can have at most 500
characters.

## 1.0.0 (build 1)

First release of Simple Body Log.
• Log your weight and up to 16 body measurements
• See your progress on trend charts and in your history
• Set a goal and track changes since your start date
• Metric or imperial units, light and dark themes
• Import and export your data as CSV
No account, no ads. Your data stays on your phone.
